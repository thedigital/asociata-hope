Our association was founded in May 2016, but the mission of saving street animals (dogs and cats) began many years ago.

  • 2016 the year the association was founded
  • 150 animals in our care at any time
  • Bucharest and the surrounding area

It all started with a kitten

This beautiful road on the one hand, but very difficult on the other hand, began when I adopted a kitten from the internet named Ivan. Shortly after, I found a mother with 4 kittens in front of my apartment building. Strangely, until then I had not "seen them on the streets", from time to time I would carry food in front of my apartment building knowing that there were animals on the street, but without "seeing them, knowing them and feeling them".

The turning point

Then a trigger happened... our newly adopted kitten had also been on the street like the 4 kittens in front of the apartment building... I didn't think much about it, I took them from there and found them families, one of them staying with us, and I sterilized the cat... great joy that I had gotten rid of the problem, except that things never go as planned. Shortly after, another kitten appeared and then another and another, then I discovered the dogs... They were also nobody's... I had never "seen" them before.

Today, around 150 animals

Once we became aware of the problem, their pain and suffering, we didn't stop, one after another... ending up constantly taking care of around 150 animals with efforts, sacrifices, monthly costs far beyond what we can bear.

There is a saying that you can't go back, so we move forward.

We need support so we can continue the mission. Any donation can make the difference between life and death at some point. Be with us, help us so we can help no one's animals. Thank you!
