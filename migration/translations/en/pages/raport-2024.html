Our mission

Founded in 2016, HOPE Animal Protection Association has as its mission to offer a second chance to homeless animals.

Our small team rescues abandoned animals (dogs and cats), provides them with care, shelter, and love, and works to find them families willing to adopt them. To date, we have facilitated over 340 responsible adoptions.

This achievement is the greatest reward for our efforts. Every successful adoption motivates us to continue the fight for those still waiting for a home.

Objectives

We believe prevention is key. That is why we promote sterilization and educate the community about its importance in reducing the number of abandoned animals.

An important goal is organizing intensive free sterilization campaigns for owned and stray animals, in order to prevent abandonment.

Achievements

The completion of the dog sanctuary construction marks a significant milestone in our mission to provide a safe, modern, and friendly shelter for the dogs we rescue and care for.

This space, carefully planned and built with dedication, was made possible thanks to the generosity and involvement of people with big hearts, whether through corporate social responsibility companies or individuals who chose to contribute through donations and sponsorships.

Every brick laid, every corner arranged was built with love and responsibility, with the purpose of offering dogs an environment where they feel protected, loved, and prepared for a better life.

175 souls in our care

  • 100 dogs living in the sanctuary.
  • 75 cats housed in two specially arranged locations, where they receive the attention and care they need.
  • Among them, 64 permanent residents: 3 blind cats, 2 paralyzed dogs, 5 dogs with various disabilities, 26 senior cats and 28 senior dogs.
