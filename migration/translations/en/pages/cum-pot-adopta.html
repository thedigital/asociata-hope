How to Adopt an Animal – Complete & Responsible Guide

Adoption changes two lives: the animal’s and yours.
Before you begin, please read the steps and requirements carefully.

1️⃣ What You Should Know Before Adopting

Adoption is a long-term commitment.

The animals in our care have experienced abandonment, trauma, or medical challenges.

They need stability, patience, and genuine commitment.

Average lifespan:

  • Cats: 12–18 years

  • Dogs: 10–15 years

Please continue only if you are ready for this responsibility.

2️⃣ Who Can Adopt

You can adopt if you:

  • Are over 18 years old

  • Have landlord approval (if renting)

  • Have time and financial resources for proper care

  • Understand adoption is permanent

We do NOT place animals for:

  • Breeding

  • Chains or yards without proper shelter

  • Surprise gifts

  • “For the children” adoptions without adult responsibility

3️⃣ Mandatory Requirement for Cat Adoptions

Cats are adopted ONLY into homes with secured windows and balconies fitted with cat-safe protective netting.

This is not optional. It is mandatory.

Why?

  • Falls from heights are a leading cause of fatal injuries

  • Standard insect screens are not safe

  • Any cat can react suddenly

Before adoption we may request:

  • Photos of installed netting

  • Confirmation of installation

  • Written agreement in the adoption contract

4️⃣ The Adoption Process – Step by Step

  1. Choose the right animal

  2. Submit the application / contact us

  3. Compatibility discussion

  4. Home visit (if required)

  5. Contract signing

Adoption becomes official after signing.

5️⃣ What Adoption Includes

All animals are:

  • Dewormed (internal & external)

  • Age-appropriate vaccinated

  • Spayed / neutered (or mandatory later sterilization)

  • Behaviorally assessed

Animals with medical conditions are adopted only with full disclosure and consent.

6️⃣ Are There Costs?

We do not charge an “adoption fee.”

However, we request a donation to help cover:

  • Vaccinations

  • Medical treatments

  • Food

  • Veterinary care

Your support helps save the next animal.

7️⃣ After Adoption

We stay by your side.

  • We provide advice and guidance

  • We occasionally request updates and photos

  • The animal must return to us if the adoption does not work out

Rehoming without our consent is not allowed.

8️⃣ Special Adoptions

🐾 Senior animals – calm, loving, grateful
♿ Animals with disabilities – capable of normal lives with minor adaptations

We provide dedicated guidance.

9️⃣ Can’t Adopt Right Now?

You can help through:

  • Virtual adoption

  • Donations

  • Volunteering

  • Sharing adoption posts
