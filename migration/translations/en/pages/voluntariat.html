Join the Hope Association team!

Our efforts are directed towards improving the lives of as many homeless animals as possible, educating the population about the benefits of pet sterilization, and fighting against abandonment and cruelty to animals.

Volunteering within our association involves several activities, including:

– help given at the shelter

– temporary accommodation of animals until adoption

– posting ads on adoption sites to find adopters

– promoting the association

– obtaining funds through sponsorships, redirecting 3.5% of income tax, as well as redirecting profit tax in the case of companies.
