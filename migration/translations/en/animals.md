### adoptii-pisici/melisa
🌷 Melisa is a rather shy, calm and very quiet cat, with a sensitive soul that needs time and patience to open up. She is not the kind to come first and ask for attention, but once she gains trust she becomes a discreet and deeply attached presence.

Melisa is ideal for a quiet home with patient people who understand that sometimes the most beautiful love is the one that is built slowly. Once won, her affection is sincere and deep 💚
